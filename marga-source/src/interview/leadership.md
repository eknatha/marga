## Tell me about a conflict.
level: mid
Use STAR: describe a real disagreement, how I listened to the other view, found shared goals and the outcome, and what I would do differently.

## How do you explain a technical decision?
level: mid
State the problem, options and trade-offs, my recommendation and the risks, in writing first, adapted to what the audience cares about. I explain the trade-off, give a concrete example from my own work and say how I would check that the change really worked.
